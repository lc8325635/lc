What is a Substitution Cipher?

什么是替换密码？

  • Each letter is replaced by another.
    每个字母被替换成另一个字母。
  • The alphabet is completely shuffled.
    整个字母表被完全打乱。
  • Much harder to break than Caesar!
    比凯撒密码更难破解！

破解替换密码的 3 种方法

1. Letter Frequencies

字母频率

'E' is the most common letter in English.

2. Common Words

常见单词
