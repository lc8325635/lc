Finding the Area Under a Curve

While differentiation tells us the gradient of a curve at a specific point, integration allows us to calculate the area under a curve between two points. Integration is the reverse process of differentiation.

The definite integral from a to b is defined as:

∫abf(x)dx=F(b)−F(a)

where F(x) is the indefinite integral (antiderivative) of f(x) with respect to x.

  • ∫ : the integral sign
  • a : the lower limit of integration
  • b : the upper limit of integration
  • f(x) : the integrand (the function being integrated)
  • dx : indicates the variable of integration
