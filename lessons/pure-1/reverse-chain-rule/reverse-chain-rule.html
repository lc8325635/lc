Integration of (ax+b)n

We know from the chain rule that differentiating (ax+b)n+1 brings down a power of n+1 and multiplies by a. We can use this to derive the integration formula.

  1. Let y=(ax+b)n+1a(n+1)
  2. ⇒ dydx=1a(n+1)×a(n+1)(ax+b)n
  3. ⇒ dydx=(ax+b)n
  4. ⇒ ∫(ax+b)ndx=(ax+b)n+1a(n+1)+c (where n≠-1)
∫(ax+b)ndx=(ax+b)n+1a(n+1)+c

Here, a is the coefficient of x, b is the constant term, and n is the power.

Find ∫(2x+3)4dx.

  1. Identify a=2, b=3, n=4
  2. ⇒ ∫(2x+3)4dx=(2x+3)52×5+c
  3. ⇒ =110(2x+3)5+c

  1. Find ∫(3x-1)5dx.

    1. a=3, b=-1, n=5
    2. ∫(3x-1)5dx=(3x-1)63×6+c
    3. =118(3x-1)6+c

  2. Find ∫1(4x+1)2dx.

    1. Rewrite as ∫(4x+1)-2dx
    2. =(4x+1)-14×(-1)+c
    3. =-14(4x+1)+c

  3. Find ∫3x+2x+1dx.

    1. Use algebraic manipulation: 3x+2x+1=3(x+1)-1(x+1)1/2
    2. =3(x+1)1/2-(x+1)-1/2
    3. =3(x+1)3/23/2-(x+1)1/21/2+c
    4. =2(x+1)3/2-2(x+1)1/2+c
